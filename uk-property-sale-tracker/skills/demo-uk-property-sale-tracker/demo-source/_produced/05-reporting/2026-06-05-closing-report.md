# Closing report  (Quantum 245, Tamworth)

**To:** Helix Real Estate Partners LLP   **Date:** 2026-06-05

The sale of Quantum 245, Tamworth **completed on 5 June 2026 at £57,600,000** to Prologis
UK, **4.7% above the £55,000,000 (5.15% NIY) quoting price**. Process: informal tender,
two rounds; six parties, five qualified, five round-1 offers, four best-and-final, sold
unconditional with a 4-week completion. TOGC, no VAT on the price. Fee invoice raised
separately. Thank you for the instruction.  Adam Moore, Director.
