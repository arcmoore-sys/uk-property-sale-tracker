This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is made between:

**Disclosing Party:** [SELLER / AGENT NAME], of [ADDRESS] ("the Discloser"); and

**Receiving Party:** [BUYER NAME], of [BUYER ADDRESS] ("the Recipient").

**Property:** [PROPERTY ADDRESS] ("the Property").

**Date:** [DATE]

## 1. Purpose
The Recipient wishes to receive confidential information relating to the proposed
sale of the Property in order to evaluate a possible offer ("the Purpose").

## 2. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by or on behalf of the
Discloser in connection with the Property and the proposed sale, whether marked
confidential or not, including pricing, financial, tenancy, valuation, and
commercial information, but excluding information that is or becomes public through
no fault of the Recipient.

## 3. Obligations
The Recipient shall: (a) keep the Confidential Information strictly confidential;
(b) use it solely for the Purpose; (c) not disclose it to any third party except to
its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis who are bound by equivalent
confidentiality obligations; and (d) not use it to the detriment of the Discloser.

## 4. Return or Destruction
On request, the Recipient shall return or destroy all Confidential Information and
any copies.

## 5. No Obligation / No Warranty
Nothing in this Agreement obliges either party to proceed with any transaction. The
Confidential Information is provided without warranty as to accuracy or completeness.

## 6. Term
The obligations in this Agreement continue for [2] years from the date above.

## 7. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales and the parties submit to
the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
